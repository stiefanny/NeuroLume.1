    RESEARCH TRANSPARENCY

    The model, its limits, and open questions.

    NoSMOTE stacking combines XGBoost, LightGBM, and CatBoost through Logistic Regression. It classifies stroke status recorded in the research data; it does not predict a future stroke event.

    Categories use two research thresholds: clinical and precision guard. They are not validated clinical risk levels. False positives and missed stroke cases remain possible.

    EVALUATION STATUS

    Independent validation is not yet available

    Hospital data must have compatible labels and definitions for all ten features. Internal experiment results do not establish performance among website users.
